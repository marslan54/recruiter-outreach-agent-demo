Recruiter Outreach Agent, run end to end

An AI email agent built with n8n. It imports recruiter contacts, writes a personalised email, asks for approval in Slack, sends it from Gmail with follow-ups, classifies every reply, stops when someone answers, and reports the numbers. This page is a recording of a full run through the real workflows, with emails caught by a local mail server and a fictional sample brand and recruiters.

What happened, step by step

  1. Import15 CSV rows: 12 new, 1 duplicate with different capitals, 1 invalid address, 1 person who had unsubscribed before. Importing again added nobody.
  2. Draft and approveOne personalised email per contact, each posted to Slack with single-use Approve and Reject links. 16 were approved and 1 rejected. Nothing was sent before approval, and a tampered link was refused.
  3. Send11 emails sent one at a time, each with an unsubscribe link and postal address. Running the sender again sent nothing twice.
  4. RepliesSix replies classified: interested ×2, not interested, unsubscribe, out of office, bounce. Each stopped that person's sequence. Interested recruiters triggered a Slack alert. A duplicate reply was processed once.
  5. UnsubscribeOne recruiter clicked the unsubscribe link and was suppressed everywhere.
  6. Follow-ups5 follow-ups went only to people who were emailed and hadn't replied or opted out. A reply after the follow-up stopped the sequence.
  7. ReportThe live dashboard and the daily Slack summary show the same numbers.
